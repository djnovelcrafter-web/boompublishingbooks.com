About the Author

Mira Lavelle

Mira Lavelle writes romance about the oldest magic there is — not being loved, but being truly seen.

These are worlds with weather in them: rooms you can smell, houses that remember, a past that has never agreed to stay in the past. Every story turns on a secret at its center and a woman walking toward a life she cannot yet see the shape of — and discovering, somewhere on the way, a strength nobody told her she had.

Expect slow-burn longing that costs something, an argument running quietly beneath the love story, and something older than either of them moving underneath it all.

The sweep is historical. The stakes are always one heart.

Mira Lavelle is the author of When I See Through You, This Version of Us, No One's Girl and the Ink & Inheritance series, beginning with The Empress of Ink. She is the romance voice of DJ Trichinapally, founder of Boom Publications LLC.

She loves hearing from readers. Visit BoomPublishingBooks.com or write to mira@boompublishingbooks.com.
