About the Author

Eveline Cross

Eveline Cross writes fantasy about what waits on the other side of the door.

Ancient kingdoms and older politics. Courts carrying a thousand years of grudge. Mythical worlds that never existed and somehow seem to remember you anyway. Every story begins with a crossing, turns on a secret buried at the heart of the place, and belongs to someone ordinary who discovers a power nobody ever told them was theirs.

Expect wonder with real teeth, found family built out of the wreckage of the old one, romance that has to be chosen rather than decreed, and a message running quietly beneath the adventure.

The worlds are vast. The hands that open them are always ordinary.

Eveline Cross is the author of the Bridge-Walker Trilogy — The Accidental Nexus, The Mirage Queen and The Convergence — along with The Living Blade and The Five Day Forecast. She is the fantasy voice of DJ Trichinapally, founder of Boom Publications LLC.

She enjoys hearing from readers. Visit BoomPublishingBooks.com or write to eveline@boompublishingbooks.com.
