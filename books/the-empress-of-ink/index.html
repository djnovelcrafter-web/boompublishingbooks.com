The Story

A lost library. A remembered love. A life she must claim for herself.

At forty-five, Beatrice Kwan has built a bookselling empire on her ability to tell the genuine from the false. But when the Ravenstone collection arrives, forged records are only the beginning. Old paper brings impossible memories: candlelight, cold stone, and a man's touch her body remembers even when her mind cannot explain it.

In Victorian England, impoverished scholar Bea Grey enters Ravenstone's forbidden library and discovers theft hidden within its ledgers. Working beside Edmund Vale, the estate's enigmatic heir, offers her something she has rarely known: a mind that recognises her own. But the intimacy growing between them comes with a dangerous question. Does he want to give her a future—or decide it for her?

Now hidden letters are surfacing in Beatrice's hands, and someone who knows her methods is using them to make her look guilty. As the threat to her reputation becomes a threat to her life, she must uncover what connects her to the woman in the library—and how much of a remembered love she dares to trust.

The truth could save everything she has built. But only Beatrice can decide who gets to write what comes next.

Chapter One

Opening excerpt from The Empress of Ink

The laid lines were wrong.

Not obviously wrong, not to anyone who had not spent the better part of two decades in an unreasonable personal relationship with Victorian presswork. At forty-five, Beatrice Kwan held the loupe steady over the title page and let the magnification do the arguing. The paper carried the faint ribs the mold had left, the manufacturing marks that should have been as irregular as breath. Instead, the spacing ran fractionally too even. Too mechanical. Too perfect for 1874.

She had the lie in nine seconds. What happened next jolted her from her work.

The restoration room went cold.
