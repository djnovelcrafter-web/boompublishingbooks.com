About Boom Publishing Books

A small press built around story worlds

Boom Publishing Books publishes darkly polished genre fiction across three linked reader promises: science fiction by Vesper Locke, portal fantasy by Eveline Cross, and romance and gothic suspense by Mira Lavelle.

The list is curated, intentional, and reader-first. Each book page points you toward the mood, themes, characters, and sample chapter that will help you choose the world you want to enter next.
